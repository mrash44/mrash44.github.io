Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

What pa-toolkit is

pa-toolkit is a personal, self-hosted tool. It is used only by its owner, with Google accounts the owner manages for their own household. It is not offered to the public.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages and creates drafts. Sends email only after the owner reviews and approves each one (or sends a summary email to a fixed household address).
  • Google Calendar: reads events and creates or updates events and their reminders after the owner approves each change.
  • Google Tasks: reads, adds, updates and completes tasks after the owner approves.

It cannot delete email, calendar events or tasks.

How the data is used

Data is used only to show information to the owner and carry out actions the owner asks for. When the owner asks Claude (an AI assistant by Anthropic) a question, the relevant email, calendar or task content is passed to Claude to answer it. Data is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models by this tool, and not shared with anyone else.

Storage and security

  • pa-toolkit runs on the owner's own computer. There is no pa-toolkit server or database.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in the operating system's protected credential store, not in plain files.
  • A local log records actions taken (such as "email sent" or "event created"), without message contents.

Limited Use

pa-toolkit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops pa-toolkit from reading or changing any data.

Contact

Questions: contact the owner through this site's GitHub repository.
